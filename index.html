About

Where software meets healthcare

I'm a second-year Computer Science Engineering student at JJCET, Karur. My interest in healthcare technology started with something simple: growing up aware of how much access to good diagnosis and care can vary between cities and rural areas in Tamil Nadu.

That's shaped almost everything I've built since — from early screening tools for oral cancer and anemia, to offline-first apps designed for smallholder farmers and rural communities with unreliable connectivity. I care about building things that actually work for the people who need them most, not just in a demo.
